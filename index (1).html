About Me

I'm a self-taught data analyst based in Lagos, Nigeria, on a focused mission to build a career in data. I got into analysis because I've always been drawn to finding the story behind the numbers — understanding what the data is actually saying, not just what it looks like on the surface.

My learning has been hands-on from the start. Rather than just watching tutorials, I've built real end-to-end projects — working with messy datasets, making deliberate cleaning decisions, designing data models, and producing dashboards that answer genuine business questions.

I'm currently expanding my portfolio and am open to entry-level analyst roles and freelance data projects where I can continue growing while delivering real value.
