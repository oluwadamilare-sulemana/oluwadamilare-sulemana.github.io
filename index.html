About Me

Who I Am

I'm a data analyst based in Lagos, Nigeria — driven by a genuine curiosity for what numbers are really saying beneath the surface. I didn't just take courses; I built things. Real datasets, real cleaning decisions, real dashboards.

My approach is always visual-answer focused — I believe a great dashboard should answer the business question before anyone reads a word. Every project I take on starts with understanding what decision needs to be made, then working backwards from that.

Python ETL Pipeline
E-commerce · Python 2026

Olist E-Commerce ETL Pipeline

End-to-end ETL pipeline in Python/pandas, extracting 9 relational tables live from Kaggle via the kagglehub API. Cleaned and profiled each table individually, resolving nulls, duplicate reviews, and multi-payment records by identifying the true composite key for payments (order_id + payment_sequential). Aggregated one-to-many tables and joined all 9 sources through a sequenced series of verified left joins — checking row-count integrity after every merge — to produce a single analysis-ready fact table at the order-item grain, with a full written report documenting every transformation decision.

Energy Markets · ML 2026

Natural Gas Price Prediction

Regression pipeline predicting Natural Gas futures (NG=F) closing price from Open, High, Low and Volume pulled via yfinance. Benchmarked Linear Regression, Lasso, Ridge and an RBF-kernel SVR tuned with GridSearchCV. Ridge and Linear Regression reached R² ≈ 0.9995, while the SVR scored negative during tuning — a useful lesson in checking fit instead of trusting a default. Final Ridge model serialized with joblib.

✔ R² ≈ 0.9995 ✔ GridSearchCV tuning ✔ Model comparison
Python scikit-learn yfinance Ridge / Lasso SVR
