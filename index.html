About Me

Who I Am

I'm a self-taught data analyst based in Lagos, Nigeria — driven by a genuine curiosity for what numbers are really saying beneath the surface. I didn't just take courses; I built things. Real datasets, real cleaning decisions, real dashboards.

My approach is always visual-answer focused — I believe a great dashboard should answer the business question before anyone reads a word. Every project I take on starts with understanding what decision needs to be made, then working backwards from that.

I've worked across healthcare and retail data, handling intentional data quality issues, building DAX measures from scratch, and producing multi-page Power BI reports paired with Word and PowerPoint deliverables.
